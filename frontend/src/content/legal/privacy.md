Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Transcendence collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use our media discovery, recommendation, review, community, and chat features.

Transcendence is an educational project created as part of the 42 school curriculum. It is not a commercial service.

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of EU and French data protection rules, the project team acts as the data controller for personal data processed by this service.

Contact: [contact email]

2. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

- Account data: username, email address, password hash, profile settings, account status, join date, and authentication tokens.
- 42 OAuth data: if you use 42 login, we may receive account information provided by 42 that is necessary to authenticate you.
- Profile data: avatar URL, biography, favorite genres, favorite countries, followers, following, and onboarding choices.
- Media activity data: reviews, ratings, watchlist items, liked or disliked media, watched media, recommendation signals, and related timestamps.
- Community data: posts, comments, likes, reports, uploaded media references, moderation status, and report handling records.
- Chat data: chat room membership, messages, message timestamps, room metadata, and online or last-seen activity.
- Technical data: IP address, browser information, device information, request logs, session data, error logs, and security events.

We do not intentionally collect government ID numbers, payment information, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

3. Why We Use Personal Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

- To create and manage user accounts.
- To provide login, authentication, OAuth, session, and security features.
- To show profiles, reviews, watchlists, community content, chat rooms, and user activity.
- To generate media recommendations based on reviews, ratings, preferences, and media interactions.
- To operate moderation tools, reports, bans, anti-abuse measures, and service security.
- To debug, maintain, test, and improve the service.
- To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Legal Bases for EU/France Users

When EU or French data protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- Contract or pre-contractual steps: to provide the account, authentication, profile, recommendation, community, and chat features requested by users.
- Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent abuse, moderate content, debug errors, and maintain service integrity.
- Consent: where required, such as optional OAuth use or non-essential cookies if they are added in the future.
- Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Sessions

We may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for strictly necessary purposes, including login sessions, authentication tokens, language preferences, and security.

We do not use advertising cookies. If analytics or non-essential cookies are added later, this policy should be updated and consent controls should be provided where required.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited personal data only when necessary:

- With hosting, database, infrastructure, authentication, or storage providers that help operate the service.
- With 42 OAuth, if you choose to authenticate through 42.
- To comply with legal obligations.
- To investigate abuse, unauthorized access, cheating, spam, security incidents, or policy violations.
- To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and the service.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

As a general rule, account, profile, review, community, chat, and recommendation data may be retained while the account is active and for up to 3 years after account closure or the user's last activity, unless a shorter retention period is technically possible or a longer period is required for legal, security, moderation, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Technical logs may be kept for a shorter operational period where possible.

When data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it where technically and legally possible.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR for EU and France users, you may have the right to:

- Request access to your personal data.
- Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
- Request deletion of your personal data where legally and technically possible.
- Request restriction of processing.
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- Request portability of data you provided, where applicable.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Some requests may be limited when data must be kept for security, moderation, legal compliance, abuse prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons, or when deletion would affect the rights of other users.

To make a request, contact us at: [contact email]

If you are in France, you may also contact the CNIL if you believe your data protection rights have not been respected.

9. Automated Recommendations

The service may generate personalized media recommendations based on user preferences, reviews, ratings, and interactions. These recommendations are used only to improve media discovery and do not produce legal or similarly significant effects.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including password hashing, authentication controls, access restrictions, and security monitoring where available.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

11. Children's Data

This service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it where required.
